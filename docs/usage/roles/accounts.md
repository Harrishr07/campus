# Accounts

**Login (demo):** `accounts@sxcce.example.edu`

**What you do:** raise fee invoices, record payments, handle concessions, refunds and scholarships.

**You see (desk):** Fees and Finance, Approvals.

## Daily tasks
1. **Invoices:** Fees and Finance > Transactions > Student Invoices; filter paid, part paid, outstanding.
2. **Record a payment:** open an unpaid Student Invoice, **Create > Payment** (ERPNext), submit the Payment Entry.
3. **Bulk fee run:** Fee Schedule; bank file: Bank Collection Import.
4. **Concession or refund:** Fee Concession, Fee Refund (approval needed).
5. **Scholarship:** Scholarship Application waiting for sanction.
6. **Fines and no-dues:** Fine Applied, No Dues Clearance.
7. **Reports:** Student Fee Collection, Program wise Fee Collection, Student Fee Ledger, Concession Register.
