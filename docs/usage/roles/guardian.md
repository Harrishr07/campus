# Guardian (portal)

**Login (demo):** `guardian@sxcce.example.edu`. No desk; portal `/campus-portal`.

**What you do:** follow your ward's fees, attendance and results.

**You see:** the same portal pages as the student (Dashboard, Fees, Attendance, Results, Circulars, Leave and so on) for linked wards only. If you have more than one ward, the portal adds `?student=<id>`.

## Daily tasks
1. **Dashboard:** `/campus-portal` for your ward.
2. **Fees:** Fees; outstanding amount and due dates.
3. **Attendance:** Attendance.
4. **Results:** Results.
5. **Circulars:** Circulars; acknowledge.
6. **Leave:** Leave for your ward.
7. **Feedback:** Feedback campaigns addressed to parents.

You cannot see any student who is not linked to you.
