# Office / Academics User

**Login (demo):** `office@sxcce.example.edu`

**What you do:** run the academic office: curriculum and registration set-up, student records, timetables, circulars, certificate requests.

**You see (desk):** Academics, Academic Governance, Communication, Examinations, Faculty, Student Services, Approvals.

## Daily tasks
1. **Student records:** Student Services > Records & Certificates > Student; filter by programme or class.
2. **Enrolments:** Student Services > Records & Certificates > Program Enrollment.
3. **Registration:** Academics > Registration > Registration Window and Course Registration (semester course choices).
4. **Timetable:** Faculty > Teaching & Timetable > Timetable; Student Services > Attendance > Course Schedule.
5. **Circular to students:** Communication > Circulars > Circular; students acknowledge in the portal. Delivery: Communication > Reports > Circular Acknowledgement Status.
6. **Certificate requests:** Student Services > Records & Certificates > Certificate Request. For a type that needs approval open the draft and click **Request Approval**; the Principal approves from the Approvals inbox and the final approval issues the certificate itself (there is no separate Issue button; the number and QR appear on the request). A type that needs no approval is issued with **Submit**. Reports > Certificates Issued.
7. **Attendance shortage:** Student Services > Reports > Attendance Shortage; Condonation Request.
8. **Pending approvals:** Approvals > Reports > My Pending Approvals.
