# Mentor / Class Advisor

**Login (demo):** `class_advisor@sxcce.example.edu` (class advisor and mentor of the CSE 2025 class; holds the Mentor role, so My students lists mentees and counselling notes can be added)

**What you do:** look after one class or a group of mentees: attendance shortages, leave certificates, counselling notes, parent contact.

**You see:** Desk: Student Services, Academics, Examinations, Faculty and Research (from your Instructor role), Approvals. Faculty portal: My students (`/campus-portal/faculty/mentees`).

## Daily tasks
1. **Your mentees:** `/campus-portal/faculty/mentees`; open a student.
2. **Add a counselling note:** on that page fill Category, Notes, Agreed action, Action due, Follow-up date, click **Add note**. Tick Confidential if only you and a designated counsellor may read it.
3. **Parent contact:** Student Services > Mentoring & Grievances > Parent Interaction.
4. **Attendance shortage:** Student Services > Reports > Attendance Shortage. **Your mentoring:** Student Services > Reports > Mentoring Activity (your own mentees, counts only).
5. **Student leave:** Student Services > Attendance > Student Leave Application (medical and on-duty leave with certificate).
6. **Condonation requests of your class** (you see the requests, and their evidence, of the students you advise only): Student Services > Attendance > Condonation Request. Grievances are not yours to see: a student raises one on the portal and the Grievance Committee handles it.

You cannot open a student who is not your mentee in the portal.
