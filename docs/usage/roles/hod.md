# Head of Department (HOD)

**Login (demo):** `hod@sxcce.example.edu` (CSE). Other departments: `hod-ece`, `hod-eee`, `hod-mech`, `hod-civil`, `hod-it`.

**What you do:** run one department: approve what faculty and students raise, watch attendance and results.

**You see (desk):** Academic Governance, Academics, Communication, Examinations, Faculty, Student Services, Approvals. Data is limited to your department.

## Daily tasks
1. **Approve requests:** Approvals > Reports > My Pending Approvals; open an Approval Request and approve or reject (attendance corrections, exam withdrawal, appraisals).
2. **Attendance Correction Request:** Faculty > Transactions > Attendance Correction Request.
3. **Workload:** Faculty > Reports > Workload Balance, Timetable Clash Checker; Faculty > Transactions > Course Allocation.
4. **Attendance shortage:** Student Services > Reports > Attendance Shortage.
5. **CIA progress:** Examinations > Reports > CIA Status by Offering.
6. **Results of your department:** Examinations > Reports > Result Analysis, Arrear List.
7. **Circular to your department:** Communication > Transactions > Circular; pick a Department Audience Rule the office set up for your own department (you cannot create Audience Rules; a college-wide or another department's audience is refused). You edit and submit only the circulars you wrote.

You see no other department's staff, students or requests.
