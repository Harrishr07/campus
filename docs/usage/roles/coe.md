# Controller of Examinations (COE)

**Login (demo):** `coe@sxcce.example.edu`

**What you do:** run the examination cycle: registrations, hall tickets, seating, valuation, results, certificates of results.

**You see (desk):** Examinations, Academics, Student Services, Approvals.

## Daily tasks
1. **Exam Session:** Examinations > Transactions > Exam Session (one per term).
2. **Registrations:** on the Exam Session click **Create Exam Registrations** (choose programme), then **Generate Hall Tickets**. Check Exam Registration and Hall Ticket lists; Reports > Exam Eligibility Withheld.
3. **Seating and invigilation:** Seating Plan, Invigilation Roster.
4. **CIA control:** Reports > CIA Status by Offering; CIA Unlock Request.
5. **Results:** on the Exam Session, **Run Results** per programme, then **Publish Results** (needs Passing Board approval details).
6. **Grade Sheet:** open a Student Result and click **Issue Grade Sheet**.
7. **Revaluation and corrections:** Revaluation Application, Revaluation Outcome; Student Services > Student Record Correction.
8. **Reports:** Result Analysis, Rank List, Arrear List.
