# Principal

**Login (demo):** `principal@sxcce.example.edu`

**What you do:** approve final decisions and watch the whole college.

**You see (desk):** Admissions, Fees and Finance, Academics, Examinations, Faculty, Student Services, Academic Governance, Communication, IQAC, Placement, Research, Administration, Approvals.

## Daily tasks
1. **Approval inbox:** Approvals > Reports > My Pending Approvals; approve or reject (scholarship sanction, record corrections, result ratification, certificates).
2. **Admissions status:** Admissions > Reports > Live Seat Matrix.
3. **Fee collection:** Fees and Finance > Reports > Program wise Fee Collection.
4. **Results:** Examinations > Reports > Result Analysis.
5. **Governance:** Academic Governance > Proposals > Academic Proposal; Academic Governance > Meetings > Body Meeting.
6. **Accreditation:** IQAC > Accreditation > Accreditation Cycle.
7. **Positions and settings:** Administration > Positions > Position; Administration > Institution > Institution Profile.
