# Student (portal)

**Login (demo):** `student@sxcce.example.edu` (CSE 2025 batch). Students have no desk; they use `/campus-portal`.

**You see:** Dashboard, Fees, Attendance, Results, Exams, Revaluation, Circulars, Certificates, Grievances, Placement, Leave, Feedback, My record, Corrections.

## Daily tasks
1. **Dashboard:** `/campus-portal`: fees outstanding, attendance, latest result (SGPA, CGPA), circulars to acknowledge, exam registration.
2. **Fees:** Fees; **Pay online** where enabled, otherwise pay at the office.
3. **Attendance:** Attendance, by course.
4. **Results:** Results; Revaluation to apply.
5. **Exams:** Exams shows registration and your hall ticket; withdrawal: **Apply for withdrawal**.
6. **Leave:** Leave to apply with a certificate.
7. **Certificates:** Certificates, choose a type and **Request**, later **Download**.
8. **Grievance, circulars, My record, Corrections** (raise a change in personal, contact or guardian details).

You see only your own data.
