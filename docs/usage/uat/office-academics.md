# UAT: Office / Academics User
Login `office@sxcce.example.edu`. Guide: roles/office-academics.md.

| # | Step | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Student Services > Masters > Student | List of students; open one |
| 2 | Student Services > Transactions > Course Schedule | CSE semester 3 sessions of the term |
| 3 | Communication > Transactions > Circular, create and submit a test circular | Saved; students see it in the portal Circulars |
| 4 | Student Services > Transactions > Certificate Request, open or create one | Draft; click **Request Approval** and the request goes to the Principal (approve it as `principal@` in Approvals, see uat/principal.md). After approval it shows as submitted with a certificate number |
| 5 | Approvals > Reports > My Pending Approvals | Opens; list shows only requests assigned to you or your roles |
| N1 | Fees and Finance, Admissions | Not in the sidebar |
| N2 | Open an Approval Request you raised, try to approve it | Refused (requester cannot approve) |
